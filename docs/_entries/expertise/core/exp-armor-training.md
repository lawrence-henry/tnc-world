    === "Basic"
        You can effectively use Light Armor. In addition, your Armor allows you to add &half; your Constitution to the Armor Protection (AP) of the armor.
    === "Initiate"
        You can effectively use Medium Armor. Your AP bonus increases to your full Constitution.
    === "Advanced"
        You can effectively use Heavy Armor. Your AP bonus increases to your full Constitution &plus;1.
    === "Expert"
        Your AP bonus increases to your full Constitution &plus;2. In addition, attacks from Armor-Piercing sources have their Armor-Piercing property reduced by half (Normally, Armor-Piercing attacks reduce your armor's effectiveness).
