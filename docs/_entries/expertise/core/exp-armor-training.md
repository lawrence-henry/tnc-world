#### :material-armor:  {#exp-armor-training}

<!-- prettier-ignore -->
!!! abstract ""
    **Type:** Utility
    **Tags:** Defense, Passive
    **Prerequisites:** None

    

    === "Overview"
        |Benefits||
        |:------:|:-|
        |**Basic:**| +1/2 Con to AP |
        |**Initiate:**| +Con to AP |
        |**Advanced:**| +Con +1 to AP |
        |**Expert:**| +Con +2 to AP |

    === "Basic"
        You can more effectively use Light Armor. In addition, your Armor allows you to add &half; your Constitution to the Armor Protection (AP) of the armor.
    === "Initiate"
        You can more effectively use Medium Armor. Your AP bonus increases to your full Constitution.
    === "Advanced"
        You can more effectively use Heavy Armor. Your AP bonus increases to your full Constitution +1.
    === "Expert"
        Your AP bonus increases to your full Constitution +2. In addition, attacks from Armor-Piercing sources have their Armor-Piercing property reduced by half (Normally, Armor-Piercing attacks reduce your armor's effectiveness).
