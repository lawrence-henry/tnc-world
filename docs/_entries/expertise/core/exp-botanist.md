#### :material-flower: Botanist {#exp-botanist}

<!-- prettier-ignore -->
!!! abstract ""
    **Type:** Crafting
    **Tags:** 
    **Prerequisites:** Trained with Natural Lore or Poison Lore

    You have a knack for using the natural world to your advantage, skillfully crafting medicines and poisons from gathered ingredients.

    === "Overview"
        **Role:** Active Support

        |Benefits||
        |:------:|:-|
        |**Basic:**| Extra Materials, Bonus Healing |
        |**Initiate:**| New Medicine |
        |**Advanced:**| Bonus Healing, Potent Poisons |
        |**Expert:**| Bonus Healing, Additional Poison Effects |

    === "Basic"
        When gathering natural ingredients to craft medicines or poisons, you gather (Wits × 2) + your Chance Die in crafting materials. Additionally, medicines you create provide a bonus of +3 Health recovered when used.
    === "Initiate"
        You can create a new type of medicine or poison with a specific effect, determined in collaboration with the GM. When you successfully gather ingredients, you may roll an additional d6 and add it to the total materials gathered.
    === "Advanced"
        The Health bonus from your medicines increases to +6. Poisons you craft now have their save CR increased by +2. Additionally, you can choose to create an antidote that negates the effects of one poison for a single target.
    === "Expert"
        The Health bonus for medicines crafted by you now increases to +9. You gain the ability to create unique poisons with additional effects, including debilitating status effects (e.g., paralysis, blindness) based on the plants and components used. Work with the GM to define specific effects. Additionally, you can choose to share your knowledge, granting an ally a bonus to their next roll related to Natural Lore or Poison Lore.
