    You possess an innate ability to lead and inspire those around you, rallying allies to achieve greatness together.

    === "Overview"
        **Role:** Active Support

        |Benefits||
        |:------:|:-|
        |**Basic:**|  |
        |**Initiate:**|  |
        |**Advanced:**|  |
        |**Expert:**|  |

    === "Basic"
        Your commanding presence inspires your allies. Allies within 10 yards who can see and hear you gain a +2 bonus to their Defense and can apply this bonus against one attack roll each round.
    === "Initiate"
        Your ability to lead extends to Initiative. Allies within 10 yard that can hear you gain a +2 bonus to their Initiative rolls, allowing them to act more swiftly in combat.
    === "Advanced"
        **Hold The Line (2 CP).** When you stand firm in the face of adversity, your allies draw strength from you. As a Minor Action, you shout encouragement, and a number of allies equal to your Willpower Ability within 10 yards that can hear you heal as though they've taken a Breather, and gain a +2 bonus to their next Attack and Damage Rolls.
    === "Expert"
        **Fire At Will (4 CP).** Once per encounter, you can activate this unique Chance Action as a Major Action. A number of allies equal to your Willpower Ability within 10 yards who can hear you may take an Instinct Action. This action can be used for an Attack, additional Movement, or a Minor Action, empowering them to respond dynamically to threats. This Instinct Action must be used before the start of your next turn, or its benefit is lost.
