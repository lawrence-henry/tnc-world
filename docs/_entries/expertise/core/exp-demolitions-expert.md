    You've made a career out of a mentality that most don't understand. Either you're right, or suddenly it's not your problem anymore. So far, you've had a good run of being right.

    === "Overview"
        **Role:** Active Support

        |Benefits||
        |:------:|:-|
        |**Basic:**| Bonus to Explosives |
        |**Initiate:**| Detonate as a Free Action |
        |**Advanced:**| Precision Blast (3 CP) |
        |**Expert:**| Combat Engineer (6 CP) |

    === "Basic"
        Gain a bonus equal to 1&plus; your Chance Die Action Rolls related to explosive devices.
    === "Initiate"
        You can detonate Explosive Devices, including Timed Explosives, as a Free Action.
    === "Advanced"
        **Precision Blast (3 CP).** You can set explosives to target specific weak points in structures, vehicles, or armor. When setting an explosive, you may ignore up to 1 &plus; half your Level (minimum 1) of a target’s Armor Protection (AP) or structural integrity.
    === "Expert"
        **Combat Engineer (6 CP).** When you detonate an explosive device, you can choose to either maximize the Damage or increase the area of effect by up to 4 yards.
