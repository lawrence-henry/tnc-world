#### :material-medication: First Aid {#exp-first-aid}

<!-- prettier-ignore -->
!!! abstract ""
    **Type:** Support

    **Tags:**

    **Prerequisites:**

    You can treat wounds with greater efficiency.

    === "Overview"
        **Role:** Active Support

        |Benefits||
        |:------:|:-|
        |**Basic:**| 1+ CD Healing, Minor Action Heal |
        |**Initiate:**| + (Wits x CD) Healing |
        |**Advanced:**| Triage Expert (3 CP) |
        |**Expert:**| Free Action Heal, Combat Medicine (4 CP) |

    === "Basic"
        Gain a bonus of 1+ your Chance Die on Action Rolls related to Healing. Additionally, Heal is a Minor Action for you.
    === "Initiate"
        When you use the Heal Action, your target regains an additional amount of Health equal to your Wits multiplied by your Chance Die.
    === "Advanced"
        **Triage Expert (3 CP).** When a character reaches 0 Health, you can use an Instinct Action to make an assessment of their condition, and extend the time they have before death by an amount equal to 1 + your Wits Ability.
    === "Expert"
        Heal is now a Free Action for you.

        **Combat Medicine (4 CP).** When you use the Heal Action, your target regains an additional amount of Health equal to your Willpower. This enhances your ability to provide quick, effective aid in the heat of battle.
