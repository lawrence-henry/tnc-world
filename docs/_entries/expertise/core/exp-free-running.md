#### :material-run-fast: Free-Running {#exp-free-running}

<!-- prettier-ignore -->
!!! abstract ""
    **Type:** 
    **Tags:** 
    **Prerequisites:** 

    You excel at remaining in motion. It's hard to slow you down.

    === "Overview"
        **Role:** Active Support

        |Benefits||
        |:------:|:-|
        |**Basic:**| Bonus to Jump, +1 Bonus to Defense |
        |**Initiate:**| Minor Action Run, Run From Prone |
        |**Advanced:**| Bounding Leap (3 CP) |
        |**Expert:**| Leap of Faith (1+ CP) |

    === "Basic"
        Your Jump distance increases by an additional 1 + half your Reflexes in yards. Gain a +1 bonus to Defense if you've moved during the current round.
    === "Initiate"
        You can Run as a Minor Action. Additionally, you can take the Run Action starting from Prone.
    === "Advanced"
        **Bounding Leap (3 CP).** You can use your jump distance to ascend or descend walls without slowing down, as long as they have handholds or protrusions. Particularly difficult maneuvers may still require an Action Roll.
    === "Expert"
        **Leap of Faith (1+ CP).** Any time you would roll a Reflexes Action Roll involving Movement, you can spend a number of CP, and add that number to your Reflexes Action Roll. (Examples: Balance Action Rolls for high-wire stunts, Reflexes Saving Throws against Fall or Blast Damage.)
