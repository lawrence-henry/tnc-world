#### :material-:  {#exp-jack-of-all-trades}

<!-- prettier-ignore -->
!!! abstract ""
    **Type:** 
    **Tags:** 
    **Prerequisites:** 

    You gather information on as many disciplines as you can, ensuring that you have a leg up in most situations where others might scramble.

    === "Overview"
        **Role:** Active Support

        |Benefits||
        |:------:|:-|
        |**Basic:**|  |
        |**Initiate:**|  |
        |**Advanced:**|  |
        |**Expert:**|  |

    === "Basic"
        Gain half your Wits as a bonus to any Action Roll you do not have a Trait for. If you do have a Trait covering said roll, gain a +1 bonus instead.
    === "Initiate"
        Use Aid as a Minor Action. In addition, if you have a Trait related to your ally's Action Roll, they gain a Major Advantage (+5) instead of the usual Minor Advantage.
    === "Advanced"
        **Beginner's Luck (3 CP).** Force your Chance Die down by a number of points up to the amount of CP spent to make your roll a Double. If this results in a Triple, ignore the CP cost of this Chance Action.
    === "Expert"
        Increase your non-Trait bonus to your full Wits Ability. If you have a Trait covering a roll, increase that bonus to a +2.

        **Master of None (1 CP).** You can force your Chance Die up by 1 point to make your roll a Double. If this results in a Triple, ignore the CP cost of this Chance Action.
