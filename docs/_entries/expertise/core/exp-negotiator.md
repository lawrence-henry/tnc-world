    A wordsmith with a natural aura of trustworthiness, you have a wonderfully masterful gift of gab. Convincing others to see things your way is just the way you live.

    === "Overview"
        **Role:** Active Support

        |Benefits||

    === "Basic"
        Gain a bonus to Action Rolls related to persuasion or deception (your choice) equal to 1&plus; your Chance Die result.
    === "Initiate"
        Your bonus from the Basic Ability of this Expertise becomes 4&plus; your Chance Die Result, as long as the resoultion you're suggesting is peaceful in nature and mutually beneficial to both sides of the agreement.
    === "Advanced"
        **Change of Heart (5 CP).** You can use a Major Action to attempt a Social Action Roll (CR 10 &plus; Target Threat Level) to shift the target's attitude toward a neutral or friendly state. If successful, the target becomes receptive to negotiation and may hesitate or delay aggressive actions, granting the group an opportunity to make requests or avoid conflict entirely. If you fail, the target is skeptical and continues whatever actions it would normally take, and you can’t use this ability on the same target again for 24 hours.
    === "Expert"
        **Hard Bargain.** Equipment purchase prices are lowered by 1/4th for you. Equipment sell prices are raised by 1/4th for you.
