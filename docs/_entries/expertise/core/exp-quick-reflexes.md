#### :material-fast-forward: Quick Reflexes {#exp-quick-reflexes}

> Needs Review and Playtesting

<!-- prettier-ignore -->
!!! abstract ""
    **Type:** 
    **Tags:** 
    **Prerequisites:** 

    You have rapid and instinctual reactions.

    === "Overview"
        **Role:** Active Support

        |Benefits||
        |:------:|:-|
        |**Basic:**| Bonus Initiative, Ready as a Free Action |
        |**Initiate:**| Prone as Free Action |
        |**Advanced:**| Always Prepared (3 CP) |
        |**Expert:**| Observant |

    === "Basic"
        When you roll Initiative at the start of combat, gain a bonus equal to your Chance Die. Additionally, once per round, you can use Ready as a Free Action.
    === "Initiate"
        You can flip yourself up or down with incredible speed, going to and from Prone with a Free Action.
    === "Advanced"
        **Always Prepared (3 CP).** As an Instinct Action, gain a bonus to your Initiative equal to 1+ half your level.
    === "Expert"
        **Observant.** As long as you are not Incapacitated, you can no longer be Surprised.
