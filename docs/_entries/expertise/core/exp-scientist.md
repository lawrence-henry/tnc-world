    === "Basic"
        All Lore tests made as a Free Action.
    === "Initiate"
        Whenever you succeed on a Lore Test, you may immediately perform one of the following:

        - Grant an ally an additional +2 bonus to their next Action Roll involving that subject.

        - Reduce the CR of your next roll involving that subject by 4.
    === "Advanced"
        Wits based Lore Traits gain an additional bonus equal to half your level.
    === "Expert"
        You can make 2 Lore Tests per round as a single Free Action.

        - Twice per session, reroll a Lore based Action Roll, with a +5 bonus to the Roll.

        - **Rapid Analysis (6 CP):** Instantly identify a partially uncovered solution to a puzzle, trap, mystery, or enemy weakness.
