#### :material-ninja:  {#exp-stalker}

<!-- prettier-ignore -->
!!! abstract ""
    **Type:** 
    **Tags:** 
    **Prerequisites:** 

    Remaining unnoticed is second-nature to you.

    === "Overview"
        **Role:** Active Support

        |Benefits||
        |:------:|:-|
        |**Basic:**| Bonus to Stealth |
        |**Initiate:**| Run while in Stealth |
        |**Advanced:**| Shadow Step |
        |**Expert:**| Shadow's Kiss |

    === "Basic"
        As long as there is cover or concealment available, you gain a bonus to your Stealth Action Rolls equal to 1+ your Chance Die result.
    === "Initiate"
        You can use the Run Major Action and move at your full Speed while maintaining Stealth, at a Major Disadvantage (-5) to the Stealth Action Roll.
    === "Advanced"
        **Shadow Step.** You can use a Minor Action to seamlessly blend into your environment. Once per round, you can use your Instinct Action to re-roll a failed Stealth test to remain hidden. You must use the new roll.
    === "Expert"
        **Shadow's Kiss.** When you cause a target to become Unconscious or Dying while in Stealth, you can use an Instinct Action to attempt another Stealth test to drag the body out of the way and remain hidden.
    
        Additionally, you gain a +3 bonus to Search and Seeing Action Rolls to detect hidden enemies, alarms, surveillance devices, traps, and escape routes.
