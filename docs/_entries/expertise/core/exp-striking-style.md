#### :material-mixed-martial-arts: Striking Style {#exp-striking-style}

<!-- prettier-ignore -->
!!! abstract ""
    **Type:** Style

    **Tags:** Melee, Offense

    **Prerequisites:** Trained in Martial Arts, Might of 2 or higher, Reflexes of 2 or higher

    **Stances:** This fighting style uses Stances. Unless otherwise stated, only one Stance can be used at a time. Stances can be switched at any time with an Activate Action.

    === "Overview"
        **Role:**

        |Benefits||
        |:------:|:-|
        |**Basic:**| 2 Attacks per Round or +2 Damage |
        |**Initiate:**| 3 Attacks per Round or +3 Damage, **Trap and Box (1 CP)** |
        |**Advanced:**| **Return to Sender (2 CP)** |
        |**Expert:**| 1 Instinct Action Attack per Round, or 4 Attacks per Round or +5 Damage, **Ultra Combination** |

    === "Basic"
        **Swarmer Stance:** Your Unarmed Attack counts as a Minor Action, allowing you up to 2 Attacks per Round.

        **Slugger Stance:** Gain +2 Bonus to Unarmed Base Damage.
    === "Initiate"
        **Swarmer Stance:** Your Unarmed Attack counts as a Free Action, allowing you up to 3 Attacks per Round.

        **Slugger Stance:** Gain +3 Bonus to Unarmed Base Damage.

        **Trap and Box (1 CP):** When attacked, instead of relying on your regular Defense, you can roll an opposed Unarmed Attack. On a success, you stop the target's attack and initiate a Grapple, and your next Unarmed Attack gains +2 Base Damage. On a failure, the target's Attack deals an additional +2 Base Damage.
    === "Advanced"
        **Return to Sender (2 CP):** On a successful Trap and Box, you can spend an Instinct Action to make an Unarmed Attack immediately. On a successful hit, deal +4 Base Damage instead of the usual +2. 
        
    === "Expert"
        **Swarmer Stance:** Your Unarmed Attack counts as an Instinct Action, allowing up to 4 Attacks per Round. This would alternatively allow 1 Attack as an Instinct Action on another character's turn, and up to 3 regular Attacks. This does not grant additional Instinct Actions.

        **Slugger Stance:** Gain +5 Bonus to Unarmed Base Damage.

        **Ultra Combination:** While using the Rapid Attack Chance Action, when you roll Doubles, gain additional, cumulative Chance Points equal to your Might or Reflexes (whichever is higher). These points must be used for additional Rapid Attacks immediately, or be lost. You can gain these points a number of times equal to your Might or Reflexes (whichever is higher) per Round through this ability.
