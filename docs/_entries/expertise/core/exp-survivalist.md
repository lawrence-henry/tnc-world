!!! abstract ""

    **Tags:**

    You have an excellent grasp on outdoor survival techniques. These skills will be invaluable should things go south, as you're convinced they eventually will.

    === "Overview"
        **Role:** Active Support

        |Benefits||
        |:------:|:-|
        |**Basic:**| Major Advantage on Navigation and Tracking |
        |**Initiate:**|  |
        |**Advanced:**|  |
        |**Expert:**|  |

    === "Basic"
        You have a keen understanding of the wilderness. Gain a Major Advantage (+5) on Navigation and Tracking Action Rolls in natural environments. Additionally, when foraging or searching for food and water, you can move up to 5 yards faster than normal without penalty.
    === "Initiate"
        You have a knack for finding more than the average Joe. Gain an additional bonus to Action Rolls related to Tracking or Hunting Food Creatures, and Foraging Plants for food equal to 2 + half your level.
    === "Advanced"
        **Always Downwind.** Gain a Major Advantage (+5) to Stealth Action Rolls in natural environments that rely on Seeing and Hearing. Additionally, gain a Minor Advantage (+2) to Stealth Action Rolls in natural environments that rely on Smelling.
    === "Expert"
        **Clean Kill (6 CP).** As long as you haven't moved more than half your speed in a round, increase your first attack's damage that round with an Archaic Ranged Weapon by +3.
