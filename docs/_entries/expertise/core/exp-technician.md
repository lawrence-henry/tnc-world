#### :material-wrench: Technician {#exp-technician}

<!-- prettier-ignore -->
!!! abstract ""
    **Type:** 
    **Tags:** 
    **Prerequisites:**

    There are few out there with technical expertise to your degree. If it doesn't work, and it should, you're the one to call.

    === "Overview"
        **Role:** Active Support

        |Benefits||
        |:------:|:-|
        |**Basic:**| Crafting Bonus, Identify Devices (CR 15) |
        |**Initiate:**| +5 Hacking/Anti-hacking |
        |**Advanced:**| +5 Disable/Sabotage, False Trail |
        |**Expert:**| 1 Auto Success (Repair, Manipulation, Hacking) |

    === "Basic"
        **Electronic Artisan.** Gain a bonus equal to 1 + half your level on tests related to crafting or repairing devices. Additionally, you can identify the basic functions and potential issues of any device with a successful Wits test (CR 15).
    === "Initiate"
        **Tech Whiz.** Gain a Major Advantage (+5) on tests related to hacking electronic devices or protecting them from unauthorized access. When attempting to hack, you can use a Minor Action to gain an additional +1 to your next test within the same turn.
    === "Advanced"
        **Security Breacher.** Gain a Major Advantage (+5) when attempting to disable or sabotage actively monitored electronic equipment. You can also create temporary distractions or fake signals that can buy you time or cover when working on sensitive tech.
    === "Expert"
        **Master of Machines.** Once per rest, you can automatically succeed on a single repair, manipulation, or hack test as a Minor Action, representing your unparalleled expertise. Additionally, when working on electronic devices, you can choose to reduce the time required for repairs or hacks by half, making you exceptionally efficient in the field.
