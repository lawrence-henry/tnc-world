#### :material-karate: Thai Boxer Style {#exp-thai-boxer-style}

<!-- prettier-ignore -->
!!! abstract ""
    **Type:** Style

    **Tags:** Melee, Offense

    **Prerequisites:** Trained in Martial Arts, Reflexes of 3 or higher

    **Stances:** This fighting style uses Stances. Unless otherwise stated, only one Stance can be used at a time. Stances can be switched at any time with an Activate Action.

    A dangerous, brutal style for those that don't mind being close to their opponents. Your elbows and knees do the job right.

    === "Overview"
        |Benefits||
        |:------:|:-|
        |**Basic:**| Push a target away. |
        |**Initiate:**| Unarmed Attack Range -1 yard, Damage +3. |
        |**Advanced:**| Whip Kick (2 CP) |
        |**Expert:**| Impact Finish (9 CP) |

    === "Basic"
        **Muay Korat Stance:** Reduce your Defense by 2. While using any Free Action or less Unarmed Attack, gain a +5 Bonus to Unarmed Base Damage.

        **Push Kick:** When you roll Doubles on an Unarmed Attack Roll, you can choose to push your target up to 2+ your Chance Die in yards, in a straight line away from you. The push distance becomes 5+ your Chance Die in yards on Triples. A target that is moved more than 4 yards in this way must make a Reflexes Saving Throw (CR 8 + yards moved) or fall Prone.
    === "Initiate"
        **Muay Chaiya Stance:** Reduce your Unarmed Attack Range to 1 yard. Gain a Bonus to Defense equal to your Might Ability.
    === "Advanced"
        **Muay Chaisawat Stance:** Reduce your Unarmed Attack Range to 1 yard. Gain a +3 Bonus to Defense and Unarmed Damage. Additionally, when you use Push Kick, as long as you have Movement and Attacks left available, you can follow the target and attack again.

        **Whip Kick (2 CP):** Choose one of three points on your target; Low, Middle, or High. Instead of dealing Damage on a sucessful hit, your target must make a Constitution Saving Throw (CR 10 + your Might Ability). Failure applies one Tier of one of these Conditions to the Target, to a maximum of 3 Tiers in any combination:

        - Low: Fear, Slowed, or Stunned

        - Middle: Poisoned, Bleeding, or Winded

        - High: Confusion, Stunned, or Enraged
    === "Expert"
        **Chai Kick (6 CP):** When a target is suffering any Condition caused by your Whip Kick Chance Action, you can deliver a devastating follow-up attack. Make an additional Unarmed Attack Roll as an Instinct Action. If the attack hits, the target takes an additional +2 Damage per Condition inflicted by Whip Kick (max. +6). The target must then make a Saving Throw (CR 11 + your Chance Die), determined by your last successful Whip Kick Height:

        - Low: Might Save

        - Middle: Constitution Save

        - High: Willpower Save

        On a failed Save, the target becomes Helpless for a number of rounds equal to your Chance Die.
