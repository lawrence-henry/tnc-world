#### :material-cog: Tinker {#exp-tinker}

<!-- prettier-ignore -->
!!! abstract ""

    === "Overview"
        **Role:** Active Support

        |Benefits||
        |:------:|:-|
        |**Basic:**| Half Material for Crafting |
        |**Initiate:**| Temporary Bonus to Weapons and Armor |
        |**Advanced:**| Improvised gadgets last 2x longer |
        |**Expert:**| I Meant To Do That (6 CP) |

    === "Basic"
        When crafting, you only use half the required material.
    === "Initiate"
        You can temporarily increase the effectiveness of Weapons and Armor by spending half of their benefit in Scraps or Chemicals, gaining what you spent as a bonus. (i.e.: you can modify a +4 AP armor, gaining a +2 bonus to the AP by spending 2 Scraps. Until the end of the next encounter, the armor counts as having +6 AP.)
    === "Advanced"
        Your improvised gadgets, weapons, and armor last twice as long as normal.
    === "Expert"
        **I Meant To Do That (6 CP):** Once per encounter, if you would fail an Action Roll, you can reroll it using a different Ability. You must be able to justify a reason for it to work, and there will always be a consequence for it. (i.e.: failed to pick a lock, so you roll with Might, push harder on the lockpicks; opening the door, but breaking both the lock and the picks in the process.)
