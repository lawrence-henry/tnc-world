#### :material-target-account: Center Axis Relock {#spc-center-axis-relock}

<!-- prettier-ignore -->
!!! abstract ""
    **Type:** 
    **Tags:** 
    **Prerequisites:** Small-Arms Expert Style Advanced Tier Ability or Rifleman Style Advanced Tier Ability, CQB Style Expert Tier Ability

**Close Quarter Ready:** As long as you are within your Firearm's Short or CQB Range, you can use Aim as a Free Action. Additionally, while aiming, you can ignore up to an additional 2 of a target's AP provided by Equipment.

**Point Blank Shooter:** While within your firearm's CQB Range, including when involved in a Grapple, your target must make Reflex Saving Throws against your Ranged Attacks, instead of you needing to roll. The CR for this Reflexes Saving Throw is 10 + your Perception Ability. On a success, they take no damage. On a failure, they take your weapon's normal damage. If they fail by 2 or more, they take your weapon's Chance Damage, using their Chance Die. If they fail by 5 or more, they take your weapon's Critical Damage, using their Chance Die.

**Trim the Wick (12 CP):** You can target a number of opponents equal to your Perception Ability within your firearm's Short or CQB Range. Roll a single Ranged Attack against the targets as a Major Action, dealing your Total Damage twice to each of them.
