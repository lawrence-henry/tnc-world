### Expert Chemist

Requirement: Trained in Chemical Lore, Poison Lore, or Natural Lore; Botanist Advanced Tier Ability

###

You are a prodigy in the lab, finding new potencies and combinations otherwise unheard of.

Gain a Major Advantage (+5) on Action Rolls related to crafting and using Chemicals, Poisons, and Medicines.

**Mad Science:** Items you create gain a +5 bonus to their numerical potency effects (such as Damage, Healing, Ability Damage, or bonuses to Action Rolls). This does not increase Condition Tiers Applied. Items using dice timers increase by +1 die (1d6 becomes 2d6, etc).

- **Compounding Prodigy (5 CP).** You can create a powerful chemical compound that provides a one-time use effect, such as a temporary boost to Ability Scores, Reduction to specific Damage types, a temporary additional Special Ability Mutation, etc. Be sure to consult with your GM to determine whether or not a compound is within reasonable limits.
