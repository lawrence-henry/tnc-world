#### :material-medical-bag: Field Medic {#spc-field-medic}

<!-- prettier-ignore -->
!!! abstract ""
    **Type:** 
    **Tags:** 
    **Prerequisites:**

Requirement: Trained in Healing, First-Aid Advanced Tier Ability

###

You're a life-saver. If you have anything to say about it, your friends are going to make it home safely.

- **I've Got You (6 CP).** When you make a Heal Action Roll, you can maximize the Chance Die.

- **Medical Miracle (12 CP).** When you use the Heal Action on a target at 0 Hit Points, as long as the target character has been dead no longer than 3 minutes (12 rounds), if you pass the Heal check, they are stabilized and restored to 1 Health, but remain unconscious until natural healing or the Regeneration Special Ability returns them to their Maximum Health. This can only be attempted once per character death.
