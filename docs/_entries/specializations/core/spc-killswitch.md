### Killswitch

Requirement: Trained in Martial Arts, Trained in Healing

###

You excel at striking where it hurts, dealing debilitating effects to your enemies at an astonishing rate.

You reduce the Sudden Death Action Roll threshold for your attacks to 8 Conditions instead of 10.

**Gentle Strikes.** You can choose to make Sudden Death non-lethal. If you do so, the target falls to 1 Health and remains unconscious until all Condition Tiers applied have been healed.

**What Armor.** You can choose to deal no damage using your Unarmed Attacks. If you do so, you may apply a single Condition Tier regardless of your target's AP.

**Body Breaker (9 CP).** On any attack with which you apply a Condition, the target suffers a Minor Disadvantage (-2) on their Saving Throw to resist the Condition. Additionally, when you apply a Condition in this way, you can apply one appropriate additional Condition of your choice.

**Kill Switch (3+ CP).** When you trigger the Sudden Death Action Roll, you can impose a -1 penalty on the target's Saving Throw for every 3 CP spent, up to a maximum penalty of -6.
