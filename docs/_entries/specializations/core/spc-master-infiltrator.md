#### :material-snake: Master Infiltrator {#spc-master-infiltrator}

Requirement: Trained in Stealth, Stalker Initiate Tier Ability

###

Slow is smooth, smooth is fast. No one is quite sure how you've gotten past them, until it's already too late to stop you.

**Blind Spot.** You can make a Stealth Check, even while observed or in direct combat. In addition, as long as you remain hidden, Perception Checks to spot you become Minor Actions, instead of Non-Actions.

**Don't Flinch.** If a character uses an Instinct Action targeting you, you may roll a Reflexes Saving Throw CR 10 + their Chance Die to resist it. On a success, that character's action fails, wasting the Instinct Action. On a failure, you cannot attempt Don't Flinch against that character for the remainder of the current encounter.

**Rapid Entry (6 CP).** You can perform one of any of the following tasks as a Free Action: Stealth (against Electronic Detection), Lockpicking, or Disarming Traps. For an additional 3 CP, you can do so with a Major Advantage (+5).
