#### :material-ninja: {#spc-}

### Master Marksman

Requirement: Trained in Long-Arms, Rifleman Style Initiate Tier Ability

###

**Cold Charlie.** As long as you take the Move and Defend Actions in the same round, you can make a Stealth Action Roll using your Perception Ability instead of Reflexes. Choose one item from your Active Inventory to leave behind as a decoy. As long as you remain hidden this way, an enemy that wants to locate or target you must make a Wits Saving Throw CR 10 + your Perception. On a failure, they attack the decoy. On a success, they may attack you as normal.

**Calculated Shot.** When you Aim with a Long-Arm, you can reduce penalties caused by Range, Called Shots, Environmental Conditions, or your Firing Position by an amount equal to your Perception, to a minimum penalty of 0. For 3 CP, you can reduce those penalties by an additional amount equal to your Wits, to a minimum penalty of 0.

**Hawkeye Radar.** When you make a Perception Check to locate a character in Stealth or firing a suppressed weapon, gain a bonus to the roll equal to your Wits. You can automatically determine the general direction and approximate distance of a suppressed shooter even on a failed roll. On a success, you locate the target normally.

**Counter-Sniper (6 CP).** You take a knee when the shooting starts. As an Instinct Action, you can use the Take Cover Action, regardless of available cover. This effective cover counts as 3/4<sup>th</sup> cover until the start of your next turn. You can use the Aim Action as part of this Instinct Action. For an additional 2 CP, you can gain a +3 bonus from Aim instead of the usual +1.
