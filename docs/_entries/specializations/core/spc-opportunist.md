#### :material-ninja: {#spc-}

### Opportunist

Requirement: Reflexes 4 or Higher, Agile Defense Style Expert Ability, Trained in Martial Arts

###

You capitalize on your opponent's misfortunes to an unfortunate degree, utilizing their hope as a weapon against them.

**Agile Opportunist.** When an ally successfully attacks an opponent in an area you threaten, or an opponent is subjected to forced movement through an area you threaten (such as with Footwork), you can make a single Attack of Opportunity against them as an Instinct Action.

**Reverse-Feint.** As a Minor Action, you can leave an intentional gap in your Defenses for adjascent opponents to use. Any opponent who makes a Melee Attack against you gains a Minor Advantage (+2) on their Attack Roll. Whether or not the opponent successfully hits, your next Melee Attack Roll against that target gains a Major Advantage (+5).

**Offhand Strike (3 CP).** This can only be activated after your first Melee Attack Roll of a Round as a Non-Action, but can be used with any number of additional Melee Attacks you have access to, at a cost of 3 CP per Melee Attack modified. Your target takes a cumulative -1 penalty to Defense and AP provided by Equipment for every additional Melee Attack modified, until the end the Round.

**Gungnir (6 CP).** Spend a Minor Action to shift your stance. For the next minute of combat, you can treat the Riposte Chance Action as a Non-Action, allowing you up to two per Round. These two Ripostes are used without spending CP, and the Counterattacks can be performed whether the opponent's attacks hit or miss.

**Void Fist (9 CP).** Choose a target that is within Melee Attack Range of you. Until the end of your next turn, you can make a single Melee Attack against that target's Unmodified Defense (9 + Reflexes), ignoring any bonuses provided by Equipment or Mutations.
