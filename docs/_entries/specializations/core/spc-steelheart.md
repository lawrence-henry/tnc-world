#### :material-ninja: {#spc-}

### Steelheart

Requirement: Hardened Skin Special Ability Mutation, or Iron Giant Initiate Tier Ability

###

You are the epitomy of defensive capability, and cannot be swayed easily.

**Okay, Bring It.** You can use the Taunt Chance Action for 1 CP instead of the normal 2, as an Instinct Action. When you use Taunt, you can use Might (Strength), Constitution (Stamina), or Willpower (Self-Discipline) instead of Willpower (Deception). This affects a number of targets equal to your Chance Die, as long as those targets can see and hear you.

**Steel Heart.** Ignore having up to 4 of your AP reduced or ignored by Abilities and Equipment. When you are within 4 yards of the area of a Splash or Blast Weapon, you can use your Instinct Action to step or fall into its area. Doing so turns it into a targeted attack against you, instead of dealing its damage to an area.

**Bloody Roar (3 CP).** For the next minute of combat, any attacks that don't target you suffer Major Disadvantage (-5). Attacks targeting you gain a Minor Advantage (+2). This only affects opponents within 10 yards of you that can see and hear you.

**Unbreakable (6 CP).** The bonus granted by the Defend Action increases to +5. This bonus is also granted to your AP.

**Juggernaut's Charge (9 CP).** You charge forward at up to twice your Movement Speed, dealing your Melee Damage to any targets or objects caught in your line of movement. Add your current AP to the Total Damage. Additionally, a target hit by this ability must make a Might Saving Throw (CR 10 + your Might) or fall Prone. Targets struck by this ability cannot use an Attack of Opportunity, even if such movement would normally qualify for an Attack of Opportunity.
