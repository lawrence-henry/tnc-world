#### :material-ninja: {#spc-}

### Style Breaker

Requirement: Trained in Martial Arts, Agile Defense Style Advanced Tier Ability

###

Your defense is your weapon. If they haven't learned yet, they soon will.

**Judge Opponent.** You gain additional information from using the Assessment Chance Action. In addition to its normal benefits, you can identify your target(s) current Ability Scores and active Expertises with the Style Tag.

**Rhythm of Combat.** When an opponent uses any Ability that uses Chance Points, roll a Wits Saving Throw CR 8 + their CP spent. Until the encounter ends, gain a bonus equal to your Wits, on any rolls made defensively against that Ability.

**Exceptional Disarm.** You can use the Disarm Chance Action for 1 CP instead of the usual 2. If you disarm a target's Ranged Weapon, instead of knocking it away from them, you can disable it by stripping pieces from it. That Ranged Weapon can no longer be used in the current encounter, unless the target takes a full minute (4 rounds) to reassemble their weapon.

**I Don't Think So (1+ CP).** After an opponent uses any Ability that uses Chance Points, the next time they attempt it, you can spend half their spent CP to make an immediate Melee Attack as an Instinct Action, minimum of 1 CP. On a successful hit, the target's CP remains spent, but the attempted Action is nullified. This counts as a Defensive Roll for Rhythm of Combat.

**Elusive Target (6 CP).** When targeted by an attack and it would hit you, choose a secondary target (character or object) within your Melee Range as an Instinct Action. Resolve the original Attack Roll against the new target's Defense.
