### Tactical Genius

Requirement: Commander Initiate Tier Ability, Wits 3 or higher

###

You are a masterful team player, taking every opportunity to turn the fight in your side's favor.

**Flank Maneuvering.** Once per round, as a Minor Action, you can choose a number of targets equal to your Willpower Ability that you or one of your allies can accurately percieve. As long as you at least two or more on your team targets the same characters, those targets' Cover bonuses are reduced by one step (Total becomes 3/4th, 3/4th becomes 1/2, 1/2 becomes No Cover).

**Leader's Presence.** When an ally is forced to make a Saving Throw, you can roll a Willpower (Self-Discipline) or Willpower (Morale) Roll CR 13 as an Instinct Action. On a success, grant that ally a bonus on their Save equal to your Chance Die. On a failure, that ally suffers a penalty to their Save equal to your Chance Die, and that character cannot be targeted by this Ability until the following Rest. You can spend 3 CP to add an additional ally to your Roll, up to a number of allies equal to your Willpower Ability.

**Gang Up.** Choose a number of allies equal to your Willpower Ability as a Major Action. For the next minute of combat, those allies gain a bonus to Attacks and Damage equal to your Wits Ability, as long as they outnumber their targets.

**Set Up.** You can pay for and transfer the benefits of any Chance Action you or one of your allies has access to as a Free Action, allowing another ally to use its benefit as an Instinct Action at any point before the start of their next turn, resolving any necessary Action Rolls. For example, if you use the Seize the Initiative Chance Action, you can allow one of your allies to use its effects, potentially changing the flow of battle.

**Coordinated Reposition (3 CP).** As a Minor Action, you can allow a number of allies equal to your Willpower Ability to move up to their full Movement Speed and use the Take Cover Minor Action, immediately. This combination counts as a Non-Action, and is in addition to any movement they already could have made or will make on the current Round.
