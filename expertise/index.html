    Chapter 4: Particulars of Expertise¶

    Traits¶

    What a Trait Represents

    A Trait represents focused training or experience, not raw talent. As such:

    • Traits do not grant new actions unless explicitly stated.
    • Traits provide a situational bonus to Action Rolls where applicable.
    • Traits may be referenced by other rules (Expertise, Chance Actions, Equipment), but are not locked to them (if another Trait could apply, speak to your GM).
    • Traits never stack with other Traits on the same Action Roll.

    A Trait is a focused area of proficiency within its related ability. A Trait placed in a Primary Ability grants a +3 bonus when making an Action Roll related to it. This bonus is only a +2 for Secondary Ability Traits. The GM will usually let you know what Trait applies to each test, but if it isn't clear don't be afraid to ask if a Trait applies.

    Starting at Level 11, you may select a Trait you already have, to increase your bonus with it by 1. This increases Primary Ability Traits to a +4, and Secondary Ability Traits to a +3.

    Variable Traits are those that don't fit cleanly into any single category. When you take a Trait in the Variable list, you can apply it to one of your Ability Scores. You must be able to justify your use of a Trait should your choice be questionable. Be sure to discuss these with your GM to determine.

    Creating Unique Traits

    If a GM or Player can justify the creation of a Trait, feel free to do so. GM's, especially, should do this for high-profile NPCs and elite enemies.

    The primary caveat, of course, is to be sure the scope of your Trait is important enough to give you an edge, but limited in scope to keep the game fair.

    Info

    Note that Traits tagged with (Weapon Group) do not give you Weapon Group training. Weapon Group Training, however, is a prerequisite for taking one of these Traits. See #character-creation, or #equipment.

    Variable Traits¶
    Athletics Executing physical maneuvers, including climbing, running, and grappling.
    Craftsmanship Producing high-quality items. When combined with a Crafting Expertise, grants an additional Minor Advantage (+2) on the Test.
    Intimidation Influencing others through threats, posture, or sheer presence.
    Light Melee (Weapon Group) Fighting with weapons from the Light Melee group, often usable with either Might or Reflexes.
    Multitasking Managing multiple actions simultaneously. Once per encounter, gain an additional Minor Action.
    Profession Performing specialized career or trade-oriented tasks.

    Archaic Melee (Weapon Group) Fighting with melee weapons from pre-modern eras.
    Climbing Scaling vertical surfaces and overcoming obstacles.
    Heavy Melee (Weapon Group) Fighting with large, high-impact melee weapons.
    Improvised Melee (Weapon Group) Fighting with handheld objects not designed as weapons.
    Jumping Covering distance through leaps and vaults.
    Martial Arts (Weapon Group) Fighting Unarmed, using trained techniques and specialized stances.

    Sleight of Hand Using tricks of movement to manipulate objects in unsuspected ways.

    Info

    The Bargaining, Leadership, and Profession Traits each multiply RP gained from monthly wages, leveling, and missions; by 2, 3, or 4, depending on Trait level and whether the trait is Primary or Secondary. The benefits from these Traits do not stack.

    Working in Multiple Disciplines¶

    Cross-Training at a Glance

    • Gain the benefits of a second Background
    • Recieve:
      • 1 Weapon Group (if available)
      • 1 Background Benefit
      • +10 RP Income
    • Can be taken twice (with expanded options at Level 11+)

    Some people career hop. Some go into dual-degree programs. There isn't a one-size-fits-all categorization of background experiences. Since the backgrounds in TNC function effectively like classes in other systems, and being trained for various applications of skill is a real-life experience, these Traits operate outside of the normal Trait rules. Like Variable Traits, these can potentially fit under multiple Abilities, and can therefore be listed under any of them.

    Instead of the normal bonus gained by taking a Trait, these options allow you to pick up an additional Background, and grant you the following from the chosen secondary Background: 1 Weapon Group Training (if available), 1 chosen Background Benefit, and their monthly RP earnings increases by +10 for leveling and monthly income purchases. If taken a second time at or beyond level 11, the PC can acquire 1 additional Background training, using the above rules, which stack with the first. Alternatively, they can gain 1 additional Background benefit from their first cross-training, and their RP earnings increase by another (for a maximum of +20). This does not affect Mission income.

    Expertises¶

    Expertise is what separates the commanders from their rank-and-file counterparts. As characters grow in their abilities, it's likely they'll specialize in mastering their disciplines. Prerequisite training required to begin training in an expertise is indicated in its block.

    Each Expertise comes in four tiers, which must be taken in order: Basic, which covers the bare minimum of training required to use an Expertise's abilities. Initiate, which indicates a moderate level of training. Advanced, which is the point at which mastery begins to truly show. And, finally, Expert, which indicates true, full mastery in a given Expertise.

    The Expertises listed are separated into categories, indicating how they would most likely be used.

    Combat¶

    Agile Defense Style¶

    Type: Style

    Tags: Defense, Melee, Reaction, Mobility

    Prerequisites: Trained in Martial Arts, No Equipped Armor

    Defense is not optional for you. Your art is your bond. Your spirit is your guide. You seek safety in the heart of danger.

    Benefits
    Basic: +2 Defense, Speed Boost
    Initiate: Improved Defense Bonuses, Improvised Cover
    Advanced: Swift-Shift (1 CP), Brutal Counter (2 CP)
    Expert: Passive Defense +5, Unique Feature - Only The Strong

    You gain +2 Defense.

    Once per round, when you are targeted by an attack, your Speed increases by 1 + ½ level (round down) yards until the start of your next turn.

    Using the Defend Minor Action while in melee combat grants you a bonus to your Defense equal to half of the Defense Bonus of one chosen opponent within your Melee Range until the start of your next turn.

    A target chosen this way becomes Improvised Cover against Ranged Attack Rolls and Area Effect attacks.

    Additionally, when you use the Defend Action, you gain a Major Advantage on your next attack roll against a target within your Melee Range. If this benefit is not used before the end of your next turn, it is lost.

    Swift-Shift (1 CP): Use Defend as an Instinct Action.

    Brutal Counter (2 CP): If an enemy misses their attack within your Melee Range due to your use of the Defend Action, you may add half of your Reflexes Ability and the target's last used Attack Bonus to your next Melee Damage Roll. If your attack already uses Reflexes, use your Willpower Ability for the bonus instead.

    Defense bonus increases to +5.

    Only The Strong: If an enemy misses you with an attack within your Melee Range due to your use of the Defend Action, that enemy takes your Unarmed or Melee Attack Damage as part of their action.

    Archery Style¶

    Type: Style

    Tags: Offense, Ranged

    Prerequisites: Trained with Archaic Ranged Weapons; Archaic Ranged Weapon Equipped

    Your arm is steady, and your aim is true. Although your weapon has been overshadowed by conventional fighting methods, that doesn't mean it can be safely overlooked.

    Benefits
    Basic: Ignore 4 AP, Increased Range.
    Initiate: +2 Aim, Faster Reload.
    Advanced: Faster Attack
    Expert: Ignore Cover

    Your Ranged Attacks using an Archaic Ranged Weapon gain the Armor-Piercing Property, ignoring up to 4 points of a target's AP.

    Additionally, increase all Weapon Range Increments with these weapons by 5 + 1/2 level (round down) yards.

    Focused Shot: When you use the Aim Minor Action, gain an additional +2 bonus on all Ranged Attack Rolls until the end of your turn.

    Rapid Reload: You can reload a bowgun as a Minor Action. All other Archaic Ranged Weapons can be reloaded as a Free Action.

    Rain of Arrows: While using an Archaic Ranged Weapon, you can treat your Ranged Attack Roll with this kind of weapon as a Minor Action.

    Additionally, you can perform the Lightning Attack for 2 CP instead of the usual 3.

    Masterful Aim: While using an Archaic Ranged Weapon, you can ignore up to 3/4 cover.

    Bladesman Style¶

    Type: Style

    Tags: Offense, Melee

    Prerequisites: Trained with Archaic Melee Weapons, Melee Weapon Equipped

    You have studied ancient weapons and fighting styles, finding a practical use for them in the modern era. A pillar of dedication, you stand strong in the face of danger.

    Role: Active offense

    Benefits
    Basic: +2 Defense
    Initiate: Parry (2 CP)
    Advanced: Deep Wounds (1+ CP)
    Expert: Cull The Weak (9 CP)

    As long as you wield a bladed melee weapon with a Minimum Might Requirement of 3 or less, gain a +2 bonus to your Defense. Heavier weapons only allow a +1 bonus to your Defense.

    Perfect Parry: When you are targeted by a Melee Attack, you can roll an opposing Melee Attack Roll with a +1 bonus to the roll, instead of relying on Defense. If you succeed, you manage to push the enemy's attack away. If you roll doubles, gain a Minor Advantage (+2) on your next Melee Attack Roll. If you roll triples, you can make one immediate Melee Attack as a Non-Action.

    Deep Wounds (1 CP): Instead of dealing direct damage, increase the target's Bleeding Condition Tier by 1, to a maximum of Tier 3.

    Cull The Weak (9 CP): While your target is suffering the Bleeding Condition, you can use this unique Chance Action. Roll a Melee Attack as a Major Action, regardless of any effects that increase your attack speed. On a successful hit, you apply 1 Tier of another Condition that could be caused by your attack, to a maximum of Tier 3, or increase Bleeding by 1 Tier, to a maximum of Tier 5.

    Boxing Style¶

    Type: Style

    Tags: Offense, Melee

    Prerequisites: Trained in Martial Arts, No Weapon Equipped

    You can float, and you can sting. Any proper fighter should be good at using their fists when possible, as pugilism is the way of the strong.

    Role: Active offense

    Benefits
    Basic: +2 Damage
    Initiate: +Per Defense
    Advanced: Hard Hook (1 CP)
    Expert: Rope-A-Dope (5 CP)

    When you make an Unarmed Attack, you can spend your Minor Action to increase your Unarmed Damage by +2.

    On any round where you have not moved two yards or more, you gain half your Perception as a Bonus to your Defense.

    Hard Hook (2+ CP): On any round where you have moved at least two yards or more, you can ignore up to 1 AP per 2 CP spent, provided by Light or Medium Armor with your Unarmed Attack.

    Rope-A-Dope (4 CP): After you have used the Hard Hook Chance Action, until the start of your next turn, any Melee Attacks targeting you that are missed by your Attacker causes that Attacker to gain Winded (T1+) Condition, the Tier of Winded increasing for every missed Melee Attack.

    CQB Style¶

    Type: Style

    Tags: Offense, Melee, Ranged

    Prerequisites: Trained in Martial Arts, Ranged Weapon Equipped

    Close quarters excite you. You've made being too close to an enemy into an artform.

    Role: Balanced Offense/Defense

    Benefits
    Basic: Activate + Aim on Initiative, Hard to Disarm
    Initiate: Riposte 2 CP, Ranged Counterattack
    Advanced: Double-Tap (2 CP)
    Expert: Moving Target (4 CP)

    You can use the Activate and Aim Minor Actions as part of your Initiative Roll. Additionally, any attempt to Disarm you is made at a Major Disadvantage (-5).

    As long as you are within CQB range of an opponent, you can use the Riposte Chance Action as an Instinct Action for 2 CP instead of the usual 4. Additionally, you can use a Ranged Attack for the Riposte counterattack instead of a Melee Attack. This allows you to use Riposte against Ranged Attacks that miss you.

    Double-Tap (2 CP): You can make a single Ranged Attack Roll count as making 2 Ranged Attacks using the same Major Action, dealing damage with both as normal. These attacks must be aimed at the same target, and you must have at least two pieces of ammunition loaded into your weapon.

    Moving Target (4 CP): When you are targeted by a Ranged Attack while in CQB range with another enemy, you can use your Instinct Action to impose a Major Disadvantage (-5) on the attack roll. If the attack misses, you can immediately move up to 2 yards without provoking attacks of opportunity. If the attack misses by 5 or less, it hits the enemy you were in CQB range with instead of you.

    Crusher Style¶

    Type: Style

    Tags: Offense, Melee

    Prerequisites: Might 2 or Higher, Trained in Strength, Melee Weapon Equipped

    Why wait for the tables to turn, when you can just hit them with a chair?

    Role: Aggressive Offense

    Benefits
    Basic: +4 Damage
    Initiate: Double Footwork Distance
    Advanced: Chair Breaker (1+ CP), Armor Breaker (3+ CP)
    Expert: Ability Damage

    Whenever you roll doubles on an Attack Roll with a Bludgeoning Melee Weapon, you deal an additional +4 Damage.

    For every CP spent into the Footwork Chance Action, double the distance you push your target. Any target moved more than 3 yards this way must make a Reflexes Saving Throw (CR 9 + half yards moved) or fall prone.

    Chair Breaker (1+ CP): You may spend up to 6 CP. You deal extra damage equal to the amount of CP spent, plus your weapon's effective AP. Doing so damages your Melee Weapon. Every CP spent causes the weapon to lose one AP Threshold until it's Completely Destroyed.

    Armor Breaker (3+ CP): Instead of dealing direct damage, you can attempt to destroy a target's armor. A successful hit forces the target to make a Reflexes Saving Throw (CR 10 + your Might). On a failure, the target's armor loses one AP Threshold per 3 CP spent, until Completely Destroyed.

    Whenever you roll doubles on an Attack Roll with a Bludgeoning Melee Weapon, you can deal your Might in Damage to one of your target's Abilities. This damage persists for a number of rounds equal to your Chance Die. If you roll triples, the damage persists a number of days equal to your Chance Die.

    Iron Giant Style¶

    Type: Style

    Tags: Defense

    Prerequisites: Hardened Skin Mutation; or Light, Medium, or Heavy Armor equipped.

    Your armor is almost a second skin to you. You've trained extensively in its effective uses, and as such are not as hindered as others.

    Role: Defense

    Benefits
    Basic: 1/2 AP to Speed
    Initiate: Stand Firm as Instinct Action, Armor is 1/2 Cover.
    Advanced: Bear The Brunt (6 CP)
    Expert: Determined Protector

    You take only half of your Armor's penalty to your Speed. Additionally, you ignore your Armor's penalty to other Reflexes-based Action Rolls.

    You can use Stand Firm as an Instinct Action, instead of as a Minor Action. Additionally, your Armor counts as 1/2 Cover for you.

    Bear The Brunt (6 CP): You can ignore Armor Piercing from all sources, including from Chance Actions, until the start of your next turn.

    Determined Protector: If an Attack targets any character other than you, you can use your Instinct Action to interpose yourself, automatically taking the hit. Targets protected in this way benefit from you as 3/4ths Cover. The target of this ability must be within 6 yards to benefit from this action.

    Knife Fighting Style¶

    Type: Style

    Tags: Offense, Melee

    Prerequisites: Reflexes 3 or Higher, Trained in Archaic Melee or Light Melee; One-Handed Light Melee Weapon Equipped

    You laugh in the face of the idiot that brought a gun to a knife fight.

    Role: Offense

    Benefits
    Basic: +2 Damage
    Initiate: Reverse Grip
    Advanced: Stick-and-Move (1+ CP)
    Expert: Between The Plates (6 CP)

    You deal an additional +2 bonus to Damage when Attacking with a one-handed Light Melee Weapon that deals Slashing Damage.

    Reverse Grip: You can use a Minor Action to flip the Light Melee Weapon over in your hand to a reverse grip, improving your control at the cost of distance and damage. While this is active, your Melee Attack Range becomes 1 yard, and you gain a +5 bonus to Attack Rolls. Doing so imposes a -5 penalty to your Damage.

    Stick-and-Move (1+ CP): On a successful hit, you impose a Minor Disadvantage (-2) on your target's next attack. If used more than once, this becomes a Major Disadvantage (-5). An attack that misses you because of this ability allows you to launch a single counterattack as an Instinct Action. Instead of dealing damage, this counterattack imposes an amount of Bleeding Tiers equal to the amount of Chance Points spent into it.

    Between The Plates (9 CP): When you perform a Called Shot, your attack forces the target to roll their Saving Throw against suffering a Condition, regardless of equipment or armor that would normally protect against the effects of Called Shots.

    Lone Wolf Style¶

    Type: Style

    Tags: Defense

    Prerequisites: Perception 3 or Higher, Reflexes 3 or Higher, Trained in Martial Arts

    Your opponents' final thoughts are generally about how foolish it was to surround you.

    Role: Defense

    Benefits
    Basic: Can't be outnumbered.
    Initiate: Bonus Defense, Rapid Attack (2 CP)
    Advanced: Dance of the Reaper (1+ CP)
    Expert: Broken Pack

    As long as you remain outnumbered, you ignore Advantage gained on attacks targeting you due to number of enemies.

    As long as you remain outnumbered, your Defense gains a bonus equal to the number of targets whose Advantage has been ignored. Additionally, you can use the Rapid Attack Chance Action for 2 CP instead of 4.

    Dance of the Reaper(1+ CP): Your movement speed increases by 5 yards per CP spent. Make a series of Melee or Ranged attacks against a number of targets equal to CP spent, as long as you can reasonably reach them with your movement, or they are within range of your Ranged Weapon. This is made as a single Attack Action Roll, compared to each target's Defense, dealing Chance Damage on each, unless you roll triples, in which case you deal Critical Damage.

    Broken Pack: As long as you remain outnumbered, you gain Major Advantage on a number of your Attacks equal to the number of targets whose Advantage was ignored.

    Master Key Style¶

    Type: Style

    Tags: Offense

    Prerequisites: Trained in Long-Arms

    Doors are not a barrier to you, so long as you have ammunition in your shotgun. Enemies don't fare much better.

    Role: Battlefield Control

    Benefits
    Basic: Shotguns gain Destructive property
    Initiate: All Shotguns Semi-Auto, Dual-Loading
    Advanced: Door-Kicker, Short-Stocking
    Expert: Quad-Loading, Point-Blank Master (9 CP), Rapid Breach (3 CP)

    A shotgun you wield gains the Destructive property, ignoring up to 10 AP when used against objects not being worn or carried.

    You can rack your shotgun almost as fast as you can fire it. You may treat a shotgun with the Single Shot property as though it has the Semi-Auto property, allowing it to fire multiple shots per turn.

    Dual-Loading: Reload a Shotgun 2 Shells per Minor Action, instead of 1.

    Door-Kicker (1 CP): You've mastered the art of transitioning from breaching to combat. After firing a ranged weapon, you can move up to half your speed in yards. This allows you to maintain momentum when breaching doors, moving through cover, or maintaining tactical superiority.

    Short-Stocking: When engaging a target within Short Range or less with a Long-Arms weapon, you gain a bonus to your Ranged Attack Damage equal to your Reflexes.

    Quad-Loading: Reload a Shotgun with 4 Shells per Minor Action, instead of 1.

    Rapid Breach (2 CP): You can fire your shotgun at an object that is not being worn or carried (such as a door or barrier), as a Free Action. This allows you to quickly move through doors or cover and immediately take your next combat action without losing time.

    MMA Style¶

    Type: Style

    Tags: Adaptive

    Prerequisites: Trained in Martial Arts

    You have trained in a variety of Martial Arts Styles, adapting what you like and discarding the rest. No opponent knows quite what to expect from you next.

    Note: Benefits from this style do not stack with their original style's benefits (for example, you can only use Basic Striking Style's benefits using MMA Style or Striking Style, but not both at the same time).

    Role: Adaptable, Offense, Defense

    Benefits
    Basic: Any Basic Style, switch 5 times per Rest.
    Initiate: Any Initiate Style, switch 2 times per Rest.
    Advanced: Any Advanced Style, switch 1 time per Rest.
    Expert: Any Expert Style, Fixed.

    Pick one Basic Tier Ability of any Expertise with the Style Tag. 5 times per Rest, as a Free Action, you can swap this ability for another Basic Tier Ability. You can only gain the benefits from one Basic Tier Ability in this way at a time.

    Pick one Initiate Tier Ability of any Expertise with the Style tag. 2 times per Rest, as a Minor Action, you can swap this ability for another Initiate Tier Ability. You can only gain the benefits from one Initiate Tier Ability in this way at a time.

    Pick one Advanced Tier Ability of any Expertise with the Style tag. 1 time per rest, as a Major Action, you can swap this ability for another Advanced Tier Ability. You can only gain the benefits from one Advanced Tier Ability in this way at a time.

    Pick one Expert Tier Ability of any Expertise with the Style Label. This ability can only be swapped during an extended Rest (i.e.: as a downtime activity). You can only gain the benefits from one Expert Tier Ability in this way at a time.

    Rifleman Style¶

    Type: Style

    Tags: Ranged, Offense

    Prerequisites: Trained in Long-Arms

    There are many weapons like it, but this one is yours. When all else forsakes you, your rifle is your unconditional friend.

    Role:

    Benefits
    Basic: +2 Attack with Long-Arms
    Initiate: Aim as a Free Action
    Advanced: Suppressive Fire (4 CP)
    Expert: Rapid Suppression (2 CP)

    Your familiarity with long barelled weapons grants you a +2 bonus to Attack Rolls with Long-Arms.

    Once per Round, you can use Aim as a Free Action.

    Puzzle Expert¶

    Type:

    Tags:

    Prerequisites:

    Role: Active Support

    Benefits
    Basic: Curious Mind
    Initiate: Pattern Recognitgion
    Advanced: Mental Cross-Reference
    Expert: Specialist

    Curious Mind. You have a knack for uncovering hidden details. When you succeed on a Wits test using a lore Trait (e.g., Natural Lore, History, Arcane Lore), the GM provides an additional piece of information on the subject. This extra information may or may not be directly related to the question at hand but will broaden your understanding of the topic.

    Pattern Recognition. You’ve trained your mind to pick up on recurring themes, patterns, and solutions. Once per rest, when confronted with a puzzle, riddle, or complex problem (including disarming traps or solving coded messages), you may ask the GM one specific question about it. The GM must answer truthfully, though the answer may be cryptic or require further thought to interpret fully.

    Mental Cross-Reference. You’re adept at drawing connections between seemingly unrelated pieces of information. When performing Wits (Research) tests during an extended or complex challenge (such as deciphering encrypted data or piecing together a historical mystery), you gain a bonus equal to the result of your Chance Die. This allows you to complete the challenge more quickly or with greater precision, reflecting your ability to cross-reference knowledge efficiently.

    Specialist. While your knowledge is vast, you have two areas of particular specialization. Pick any two of your lore Traits. When making tests using those Expertise, you can re-roll a failed test and choose the result of either roll.

    Specializations¶

    A single specialist could potentially turn the tide of any battle. These abilities are usually the culmination of years of specialized training.

    Assassin¶

    Type: Offensive

    Tags: Stealth, Assassination, Burst-Damage, Target-Marking

    Prerequisites: Trained in Stealth, Trained in Light Melee, Stalker Advanced Tier Ability

    As long as the character remains undetected, they gain a +2 bonus to their Damage. If using a Light Melee Weapon, or a Ranged Weapon with a Suppressor, the +2 bonus instead becomes +5.

    Marked for Death (12 CP): Choose a target character that you can accurately percieve. For a number of Rests equal to your Wits Ability, gain a Major Advantage on all Rolls opposed to that target, except Attack Rolls. If you strike the target during this time, you may deal Chance Damage without rolling Doubles. If you do roll natural Doubles, deal Critical Damage against that target. If you roll natural Triples, deal twice your Critical Damage against that target.

    Assassinate (18 CP): Choose a target who is unaware of your presence. Make an Attack Roll as a Minor Action against that target. If you successfully hit, your chosen target makes a Coup de Grace Save with a CR 12 + your Chance Die.

    Blade Master¶

    Type: Offensive

    Tags: Melee, Bladed Weapons, Stances, Grappling

    Prerequisites: Bladesman Expert Tier Ability, Wrestling Advanced Tier Ability

    You make blade work look deceptively easy, shifting effortlessly between specialized fighting stances to control the distance, tempo, and flow of an engagement.

    Only one Stance can be used at a time.

    Half-Sword Maneuvering Stance: As a Minor Action, you can shift your grip on an appropriate bladed weapon. While half-swording, your Melee Attack Range is reduced to 1 yard. Your attacks may deal Slashing, Piercing, or Bludgeoning Damage instead of their normal Damage Type, and ignore up to 4 AP. Additionally, you gain Major Advantage (+5) on Grappling Action Rolls in which you can use the weapon for leverage.

    Bladestorm Stance: As a Minor Action, you can shift your stance to loosen up for more speed. Starting on your next turn, make your Melee Weapon Attacks as Free Actions. You can make a maximum of 3 Melee Weapon Attacks this way on your turn before using Chance Actions.

    Moving Mountain Stance: As a Minor Action, you can shift your stance, withdrawing your blade closer to your body to focus your defense. While in Moving Mountain Stance, your Melee Attack Range is reduced to 1 yard. You gain a bonus to Defense equal to your Weapon's Base Damage. You can use the Riposte Chance Action for 2 CP instead of the usual 4.

    Follow-Up Finisher (6 CP): When a target is moved by the Footwork Chance Action, you can spend an Instinct Action to follow the target and make a single Melee Attack with Major Advantage (+5). Gain +2 to your Total Damage for each Condition Tier currently affecting the target. If you are using any Stances when you use this unique Chance Action, that Stance ends.

    Center Axis Relock¶

    Type: Tags: Prerequisites: Small-Arms Expert Style Advanced Tier Ability or Rifleman Style Advanced Tier Ability, CQB Style Expert Tier Ability

    Close Quarter Ready: As long as you are within your Firearm's Short or CQB Range, you can use Aim as a Free Action. Additionally, while aiming, you can ignore up to an additional 2 of a target's AP provided by Equipment.

    Point Blank Shooter: While within your firearm's CQB Range, including when involved in a Grapple, your target must make Reflex Saving Throws against your Ranged Attacks, instead of you needing to roll. The CR for this Reflexes Saving Throw is 10 + your Perception Ability. On a success, they take no damage. On a failure, they take your weapon's normal damage. If they fail by 2 or more, they take your weapon's Chance Damage, using their Chance Die. If they fail by 5 or more, they take your weapon's Critical Damage, using their Chance Die.

    Trim the Wick (12 CP): You can target a number of opponents equal to your Perception Ability within your firearm's Short or CQB Range. Roll a single Ranged Attack against the targets as a Major Action, dealing your Total Damage twice to each of them.

    Killswitch¶

    Requirement: Trained in Martial Arts, Trained in Healing

    ¶

    You excel at striking where it hurts, dealing debilitating effects to your enemies at an astonishing rate.

    You reduce the Sudden Death Action Roll threshold for your attacks to 8 Conditions instead of 10.

    Gentle Strikes. You can choose to make Sudden Death non-lethal. If you do so, the target falls to 1 Health and remains unconscious until all Condition Tiers applied have been healed.

    What Armor. You can choose to deal no damage using your Unarmed Attacks. If you do so, you may apply a single Condition Tier regardless of your target's AP.

    Body Breaker (9 CP). On any attack with which you apply a Condition, the target suffers a Minor Disadvantage (-2) on their Saving Throw to resist the Condition. Additionally, when you apply a Condition in this way, you can apply one appropriate additional Condition of your choice.

    Kill Switch (3+ CP). When you trigger the Sudden Death Action Roll, you can impose a -1 penalty on the target's Saving Throw for every 3 CP spent, up to a maximum penalty of -6.

    Master Infiltrator¶

    Requirement: Trained in Stealth, Stalker Initiate Tier Ability

    ¶

    Slow is smooth, smooth is fast. No one is quite sure how you've gotten past them, until it's already too late to stop you.

    Blind Spot. You can make a Stealth Check, even while observed or in direct combat. In addition, as long as you remain hidden, Perception Checks to spot you become Minor Actions, instead of Non-Actions.

    Don't Flinch. If a character uses an Instinct Action targeting you, you may roll a Reflexes Saving Throw CR 10 + their Chance Die to resist it. On a success, that character's action fails, wasting the Instinct Action. On a failure, you cannot attempt Don't Flinch against that character for the remainder of the current encounter.

    Rapid Entry (6 CP). You can perform one of any of the following tasks as a Free Action: Stealth (against Electronic Detection), Lockpicking, or Disarming Traps. For an additional 3 CP, you can do so with a Major Advantage (+5).

    Master Marksman¶

    Requirement: Trained in Long-Arms, Rifleman Style Initiate Tier Ability

    ¶

    Cold Charlie. As long as you take the Move and Defend Actions in the same round, you can make a Stealth Action Roll using your Perception Ability instead of Reflexes. Choose one item from your Active Inventory to leave behind as a decoy. As long as you remain hidden this way, an enemy that wants to locate or target you must make a Wits Saving Throw CR 10 + your Perception. On a failure, they attack the decoy. On a success, they may attack you as normal.

    Calculated Shot. When you Aim with a Long-Arm, you can reduce penalties caused by Range, Called Shots, Environmental Conditions, or your Firing Position by an amount equal to your Perception, to a minimum penalty of 0. For 3 CP, you can reduce those penalties by an additional amount equal to your Wits, to a minimum penalty of 0.

    Hawkeye Radar. When you make a Perception Check to locate a character in Stealth or firing a suppressed weapon, gain a bonus to the roll equal to your Wits. You can automatically determine the general direction and approximate distance of a suppressed shooter even on a failed roll. On a success, you locate the target normally.

    Counter-Sniper (6 CP). You take a knee when the shooting starts. As an Instinct Action, you can use the Take Cover Action, regardless of available cover. This effective cover counts as 3/4th cover until the start of your next turn. You can use the Aim Action as part of this Instinct Action. For an additional 2 CP, you can gain a +3 bonus from Aim instead of the usual +1.

    Opportunist¶

    Requirement: Reflexes 4 or Higher, Agile Defense Style Expert Ability, Trained in Martial Arts

    ¶

    You capitalize on your opponent's misfortunes to an unfortunate degree, utilizing their hope as a weapon against them.

    Agile Opportunist. When an ally successfully attacks an opponent in an area you threaten, or an opponent is subjected to forced movement through an area you threaten (such as with Footwork), you can make a single Attack of Opportunity against them as an Instinct Action.

    Reverse-Feint. As a Minor Action, you can leave an intentional gap in your Defenses for adjascent opponents to use. Any opponent who makes a Melee Attack against you gains a Minor Advantage (+2) on their Attack Roll. Whether or not the opponent successfully hits, your next Melee Attack Roll against that target gains a Major Advantage (+5).

    Offhand Strike (3 CP). This can only be activated after your first Melee Attack Roll of a Round as a Non-Action, but can be used with any number of additional Melee Attacks you have access to, at a cost of 3 CP per Melee Attack modified. Your target takes a cumulative -1 penalty to Defense and AP provided by Equipment for every additional Melee Attack modified, until the end the Round.

    Gungnir (6 CP). Spend a Minor Action to shift your stance. For the next minute of combat, you can treat the Riposte Chance Action as a Non-Action, allowing you up to two per Round. These two Ripostes are used without spending CP, and the Counterattacks can be performed whether the opponent's attacks hit or miss.

    Void Fist (9 CP). Choose a target that is within Melee Attack Range of you. Until the end of your next turn, you can make a single Melee Attack against that target's Unmodified Defense (9 + Reflexes), ignoring any bonuses provided by Equipment or Mutations.

    Sifu¶

    Requirement: Trained in Martial Arts, one Melee Fighting Style of your choice at Advanced Tier or above, Free-Running Initiate Ability

    ¶

    You are a master of using your environment as a weapon, unleashing devastating damage at a surprising rate.

    Environment Master. You can strike or throw any light, loose object and send it flying toward an enemy as a Melee Attack Roll, using Thrown Weapon Range. The item cannot weigh more than 5 pounds. If it hits, it deals your Improvised Melee Weapons Damage, with damage type determined by its material. Items used this way automatically lose AP Value equal to your Chance Die.

    In My Hands. You can Ready a weapon for use as an Instinct Action. Improvised Melee Weapons gain a +2 bonus to their Base Damage and AP while you wield them.

    Ladder Work. Enemies in Melee Range while you have an Improvised Melee Weapon in your hands suffer a -2 penalty to Defense and AP.

    Ranged Trip (3 CP). You can kick an object across the floor or ground at a target as part of Environmental Master. Instead of dealing damage to either the target or object, the target makes a Reflexes Saving Throw CR 12 + your Chance Die. On a success, the target gains one Tier of Slowed until the start of their next turn. On a failure, the target is knocked prone.

    Finishing Combo (6 CP). When you successfully use the Devastating Combination Chance Action, or your target is suffering 3 or more Conditions, you can use an Instinct Action to make a single Unarmed Attack Roll. Instead of dealing damage, your target makes a Constitution Saving Throw (CR 10 + your Chance Die or number of current Conditions suffered, whichever is higher) or fall Unconscious. For an additional 6 CP, the target falls to 0 Health and begins Dying.

    Steelheart¶

    Requirement: Hardened Skin Special Ability Mutation, or Iron Giant Initiate Tier Ability

    ¶

    You are the epitomy of defensive capability, and cannot be swayed easily.

    Okay, Bring It. You can use the Taunt Chance Action for 1 CP instead of the normal 2, as an Instinct Action. When you use Taunt, you can use Might (Strength), Constitution (Stamina), or Willpower (Self-Discipline) instead of Willpower (Deception). This affects a number of targets equal to your Chance Die, as long as those targets can see and hear you.

    Steel Heart. Ignore having up to 4 of your AP reduced or ignored by Abilities and Equipment. When you are within 4 yards of the area of a Splash or Blast Weapon, you can use your Instinct Action to step or fall into its area. Doing so turns it into a targeted attack against you, instead of dealing its damage to an area.

    Bloody Roar (3 CP). For the next minute of combat, any attacks that don't target you suffer Major Disadvantage (-5). Attacks targeting you gain a Minor Advantage (+2). This only affects opponents within 10 yards of you that can see and hear you.

    Unbreakable (6 CP). The bonus granted by the Defend Action increases to +5. This bonus is also granted to your AP.

    Juggernaut's Charge (9 CP). You charge forward at up to twice your Movement Speed, dealing your Melee Damage to any targets or objects caught in your line of movement. Add your current AP to the Total Damage. Additionally, a target hit by this ability must make a Might Saving Throw (CR 10 + your Might) or fall Prone. Targets struck by this ability cannot use an Attack of Opportunity, even if such movement would normally qualify for an Attack of Opportunity.

    Style Breaker¶

    Requirement: Trained in Martial Arts, Agile Defense Style Advanced Tier Ability

    ¶

    Your defense is your weapon. If they haven't learned yet, they soon will.

    Judge Opponent. You gain additional information from using the Assessment Chance Action. In addition to its normal benefits, you can identify your target(s) current Ability Scores and active Expertises with the Style Tag.

    Rhythm of Combat. When an opponent uses any Ability that uses Chance Points, roll a Wits Saving Throw CR 8 + their CP spent. Until the encounter ends, gain a bonus equal to your Wits, on any rolls made defensively against that Ability.

    Exceptional Disarm. You can use the Disarm Chance Action for 1 CP instead of the usual 2. If you disarm a target's Ranged Weapon, instead of knocking it away from them, you can disable it by stripping pieces from it. That Ranged Weapon can no longer be used in the current encounter, unless the target takes a full minute (4 rounds) to reassemble their weapon.

    I Don't Think So (1+ CP). After an opponent uses any Ability that uses Chance Points, the next time they attempt it, you can spend half their spent CP to make an immediate Melee Attack as an Instinct Action, minimum of 1 CP. On a successful hit, the target's CP remains spent, but the attempted Action is nullified. This counts as a Defensive Roll for Rhythm of Combat.

    Elusive Target (6 CP). When targeted by an attack and it would hit you, choose a secondary target (character or object) within your Melee Range as an Instinct Action. Resolve the original Attack Roll against the new target's Defense.

    Systema¶

    Requirement: Knife Fighting Style Initiate Tier Ability, Small-Arms Expert Style Initiate Tier Ability

    ¶

    You fight with the best of both worlds, with a knife in one hand, and a gun in the other. This makes you a significant force to be reckoned with.

    Rapid Transition. Ready and Activate are Non-Actions for you, allowing them to be performed alongside other actions.

    Roll With It. You can use Melee or Grapple Chance Actions with a successful Ranged Attack Roll. In addition, When you use the Footwork Chance Action, you can either double the distance you or your target moves, or you can move both yourself and your target the same amount of distance.

    Danger Close. While equipped with both a Light Melee Weapon and a Small-Arm, you can still use techniques, Abilities, and Chance Actions, that would ordinarily require a free hand (such as Grapple or Disarm).

    Secondary Strike. As long as you are wielding a Light Melee Weapon and a Small-Arm Weapon without the Two-Handed Property, on a successful hit with either weapon, you can use a Free Action to make an additional attack with your remaining weapon. You must still be within a reasonable Range for the Attack to hit, and your firearm must still contain ammunition for each of its Ranged Attacks. You can spend 3 CP for additional attacks, up to a maximum of additional attacks equal to your Reflexes Ability.

    Wing Shooter (1 CP). When you use Aim, you can choose a number of targets up to your Perception as a Non-Action. Your Aim bonus applies against those targets for the next minute of combat.

    Tactical Genius¶

    Requirement: Commander Initiate Tier Ability, Wits 3 or higher

    ¶

    You are a masterful team player, taking every opportunity to turn the fight in your side's favor.

    Flank Maneuvering. Once per round, as a Minor Action, you can choose a number of targets equal to your Willpower Ability that you or one of your allies can accurately percieve. As long as you at least two or more on your team targets the same characters, those targets' Cover bonuses are reduced by one step (Total becomes 3/4th, 3/4th becomes 1/2, 1/2 becomes No Cover).

    Leader's Presence. When an ally is forced to make a Saving Throw, you can roll a Willpower (Self-Discipline) or Willpower (Morale) Roll CR 13 as an Instinct Action. On a success, grant that ally a bonus on their Save equal to your Chance Die. On a failure, that ally suffers a penalty to their Save equal to your Chance Die, and that character cannot be targeted by this Ability until the following Rest. You can spend 3 CP to add an additional ally to your Roll, up to a number of allies equal to your Willpower Ability.

    Gang Up. Choose a number of allies equal to your Willpower Ability as a Major Action. For the next minute of combat, those allies gain a bonus to Attacks and Damage equal to your Wits Ability, as long as they outnumber their targets.

    Set Up. You can pay for and transfer the benefits of any Chance Action you or one of your allies has access to as a Free Action, allowing another ally to use its benefit as an Instinct Action at any point before the start of their next turn, resolving any necessary Action Rolls. For example, if you use the Seize the Initiative Chance Action, you can allow one of your allies to use its effects, potentially changing the flow of battle.

    Coordinated Reposition (3 CP). As a Minor Action, you can allow a number of allies equal to your Willpower Ability to move up to their full Movement Speed and use the Take Cover Minor Action, immediately. This combination counts as a Non-Action, and is in addition to any movement they already could have made or will make on the current Round.
